Our Craft & Heritage

Mastering the Art of Specialty Coffee

Founded in 2018, AURA Roastery was born out of a relentless passion to connect coffee enthusiasts directly with high-altitude micro-lot farmers.

100% Direct Trade

We bypass traditional commodity brokers to pay farm owners 30% above Fair Trade minimums in Ethiopia, Guatemala, and Sumatra.

Small-Batch Roasting

Every roast profile is dialed in using custom Diedrich infrared roasters to unlock intricate tasting notes from jasmine floral to dark chocolate.

Zero-Waste Goal

From 100% compostable retail bags to upcycling spent espresso grounds into local organic garden compost.
